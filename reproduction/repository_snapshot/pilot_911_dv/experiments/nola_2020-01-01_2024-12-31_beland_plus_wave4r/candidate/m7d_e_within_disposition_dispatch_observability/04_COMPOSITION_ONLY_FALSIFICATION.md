# Composition-only falsification

Under the restricted composition-only model, $\Delta H01_r$ and $\Delta H11_r$ must have the same sign for a fixed category and bin.

For GOA, RTF, and NAT in both B6 and B7, $\Delta H01_r>0$ while $\Delta H11_r<0$. All six products are therefore negative, so the finite-file composition-only model is algebraically `FALSIFIED_FOR_CELL` in every primary supported category-bin cell.

This is an exact sign witness, not a statistical rejection test. UNF has zero changes and supplies no sign witness.
