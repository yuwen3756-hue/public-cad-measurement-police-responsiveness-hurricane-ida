# Identification implications

The late Ida public-topology redistribution is primarily associated with changes in dispatch-field observability within the same recorded disposition categories. Changing disposition composition alone cannot produce the observed opposite-signed $H01/H11$ changes in the six supported primary cells.

This does not identify physical dispatch, officer behavior, deprioritization, response time, queue pressure, capacity, service quality, victim experience, incidence, or causality. Public timestamp presence and recorded disposition remain administrative data-production objects.
